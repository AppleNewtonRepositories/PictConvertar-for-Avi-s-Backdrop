The app should be easy to use. Obviously, it is not fully automatic, so here is a simple instruction on how to use it:

1. How to prepare the picture for this app?
   
- The best way is to have a nice picture (b&w or colour), in JPG or PNG format.
- The best resolutions are described in the Picture Orientation section (320x365px for Vertical, and 435x250 for Horizontal), but you can use different resolutions. Try to keep the same aspect ratios for each orientation, tho. If not, the app will crop your picture (see in the Preview section).
- You can have separate resolutions for vertical and horizontal pictures.
- Do not worry if your picture is in colour - the app will convert it always to 16 grey b&w.
  
2. Generating the PKG for Newton
   
- Drag and drop your prepared picture on the app window.
- Select your orientation.
- Enter the package name (12 characters max - it is possible to use a longer name, but that can cause some issues that I couldn’t solve)
- You do not need to change anything in the Advanced section, but feel free to experiment with it. Those functions I used for testing the alpha versions of this app, but in the end, I left them in the app. Just for fun ;D
- On the right side of the window, you can see the Preview. If your picture have different resolution and/or aspect ratio, the app will crop it and change the resolution automatically. There is no option for manual cropping. Maybe in a future version.
- When you are happy with the results, click Generate Package button. The app will ask you where you want to save the package for Newton.
- Done! Now you need to install the package on your Newton. Avi’s Backdrop will see this package as a picture, so you can use it as your wallpaper.

The app should work on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs, with Tahoe macOS.
